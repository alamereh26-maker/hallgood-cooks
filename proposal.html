The Proposal

The Proposal (2009) is a romantic comedy about Margaret Tate (Sandra Bullock), a high-powered editor facing deportation to Canada, who forces her assistant, Andrew Paxton (Ryan Reynolds), to marry her to keep her visa. Andrew agrees under pressure but demands a promotion and a trip to his eccentric family in Sitka, Alaska. Suspicious immigration officials investigate, while the couple navigates a fake engagement, leading to genuine romance.
