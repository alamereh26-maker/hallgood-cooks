Love Actually

Love Actaully is most definitly should be on your christmas movies must watch list. Nine intertwined journeys examine the complexities of the one emotion that connects all individuals, love. Among these individuals is David, the handsome, newly elected British prime minister who falls for a junior staffer. Sarah is a graphic designer whose devotion to her mentally ill brother complicates her love life. Harry, a married man, is swept off his feet by his new secretary.
